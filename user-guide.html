DueDock User Guide

Your personal financial planning companion that helps you track bills and income

What is DueDock?

DueDock is a simple app that helps you see exactly which bills are due and when they're due. It shows you your upcoming bills and income so you can plan ahead and avoid missing payments.

The main goal: Help you organize your bills and income in one place.

Core Concepts

📅 Forward-Looking Planning

DueDock focuses on what's coming next. It shows you:

  • When your next paycheck arrives
  • Which bills are due before then
  • How much those bills total

🎯 The Coverage Check

This is DueDock's main feature. The Coverage Check shows you:

  • Total amount of bills due before your next income
  • Your next income amount and when you'll receive it

The Coverage Check displays two key numbers:

  1. Total bills due: $X
  2. Next income: $Y on [date]

💡 Planning Period

Use the Coverage Check to plan ahead:

  • See which bills fall within your current pay period
  • If you have multiple income sources, use the plus/minus buttons to switch between pay periods

🔍 Bill Management

Manage bills directly from the dashboard using swipe gestures:

Swipe Right on a bill:

  • Dock Full (Red): Pay the full remaining amount
  • Custom (Orange): Pay a custom amount

Swipe Right on a variable bill (no amount set yet):

  • Update Amount (Orange): Enter the actual bill amount
  • Custom (Orange): Pay a custom amount

Swipe Left on a bill:

  • Edit (Blue): Update bill details
  • Delete (Red): Remove the bill

Bill Status:

  • Regular bills show in standard text color

Long-Press Context Menu:

🗓️💳 Installment Payments

For big bills you can't pay all at once:

  • Divide and Conquer: Set up automatic partial payments that align with your income frequency.
  • Interactive Dual-View Progress Bar: The dashboard features a premium progress bar that tracks your installments in real-time.
    • Single Installment View (Default): Focuses on the current payment period, showing exactly what you've paid towards today's target (e.g., "$50 of $100").
    • Full Plan View: Tap the progress bar to switch to a bird's-eye view of your entire multi-payment plan, complete with numbered step markers.
  • Clean Labeling: We've removed redundant terminology like "remaining" and "installment" to keep your dashboard focused on the raw numbers and your progress.
  • Surplus Motivation: When you choose to pay more than your target, DueDock acknowledges your accelerated progress with a ✨ Surplus Badge on your dashboard.
  • Include in Coverage: Installments are intelligently included in your Coverage Check, ensuring you have the money set aside before your next payday.

📱 Same-Day Conflicts

Sometimes bills are due on the same day as your income. DueDock shows these as conflicts because pre-authorized payments often require funds to be in your account the day before the payment is actually withdrawn.

This creates uncertainty: if you pay a bill on the same day you receive income, the payment might not clear in time for the withdrawal.

DueDock shows these as conflicts and lets you decide which to include in totals. Use a toggle to include pre-authorized payments when you're confident the timing will work.

🔄 Variable Bills

Common Questions

Q: How is DueDock different from other budget apps?

A: DueDock focuses on upcoming bills and income rather than tracking past spending. It's designed to help you plan ahead.

Q: What if I have irregular income?

A: DueDock handles different pay frequencies (weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, and more). Just enter when you expect to get paid.

Q: Is my financial information secure?

A: Yes! DueDock stores everything on your device only. No cloud storage, no data sharing, complete privacy.

Q: Can I use DueDock for business expenses?

A: DueDock is designed for personal finances, but many users track small business expenses alongside personal ones.

Q: What if I miss a bill?

A: DueDock will show it in red as overdue with prominent visual indicators. You can still mark it as paid and the app will update your calculations.

Q: How do I try the app before entering my data?

A: Use Demo Mode from the About section on the dashboard. It loads sample data so you can explore all features risk-free.
